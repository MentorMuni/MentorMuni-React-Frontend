Straight answers

What you're going to ask in the first meeting

You've bought training tools before. Some of them are still sitting unused. Here are the answers before you have to ask.

?“How much work is this for my team?”

Upload your student list once. We handle onboarding, reminders and chasing. TPOs on live campuses spend a median of 6 hours a week, mostly reading reports. No invigilation, no scheduling, no manual result entry.

?“My students won't actually log in.”

The honest number: 71% weekly active across live campuses, and it holds because the plan is 40 minutes a day, not a 200-hour course. We report engagement weekly — if a department drops below 50%, you'll know before it becomes a problem.

?“What does it cost, and who pays?”

Campus licence is priced per enrolled student per year, and lands between ₹400–₹900 depending on cohort size. Most colleges fund it from the training budget; some split it with students. We'll give you a written quote in the first call, not a discovery process.

?“Does this help my NAAC / NBA file?”

Yes — and this is usually what gets it approved. One-click export of training hours, student progression, placement outcomes and department-wise attainment, formatted for accreditation reporting. Your Registrar stops being an obstacle.

?“Which colleges near me use it?”

We'll name them on the call and connect you directly with a TPO who has run a full season — no sales rep on the line. If nobody in your state is live yet, we'll tell you that too.

?“I'm not signing campus-wide cold.”

Don't. Run one department for one semester, priced pro-rata. If readiness in that branch doesn't move measurably against your other departments, walk away — no renewal clause, no lock-in.

Questions

The things you're actually wondering

Will this actually get me placed?
No tool can promise that — anyone who does is selling you something. What we can show is that students who reach 85% readiness convert interviews at roughly three times the rate of those below 60%. We measure the thing that correlates with offers and make it move. The interview is still yours to pass.
My drive is in 4 weeks, not 90 days.
Then the plan compresses. Tell us the date and it reweights toward the rounds with the highest return in the time you have — usually aptitude speed and HR, because those move fastest.
My college already runs placement training.
Most do, and it's usually a few group sessions. This is the daily individual layer underneath — your gaps, your plan, unlimited practice at 1am. Your college may already fund it, so check before you pay.
Are the AI mock interviews actually realistic?
They ask follow-ups based on what you just said, which is the part that catches people out. They're not a replacement for a human panel and we don't claim otherwise — they're for the twenty reps before it, which nobody else gives you.
I'm from a tier-3 college. Does that matter?
For eligibility, sometimes — that's set by the company. For everything you control, no. About 40% of our placed students come from colleges with no dedicated placement cell.
What if my score doesn't improve?
Complete the daily plan for 30 days and if your readiness hasn't moved at all, write to us and we'll refund in full. That's rare, and it usually means the plan was wrong — which we want to know.
